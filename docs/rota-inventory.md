## 2. The Day Plan (automatic — who's home + the day's texture)

Every day shows who's home (editable in Household settings) and a themed note:

| Day | Default presence | Day's texture |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Both home (Marcus WFH) | Grooming day — bath fortnight, nails, ears |
| Tuesday | Marcus office, Chesa maybe | Calm alone-time practice in short doses |
| Wednesday | Marcus office, Chesa home | Chesa's one-on-one training & enrichment day |
| Thursday | Marcus office, Chesa maybe | Enrichment toys and licky mats earn their keep |
| Friday | Both home (Marcus WFH) | Lunchtime play session, wind-down to weekend |
| Saturday | Both home | Flexible — errands with Wobbles or bigger park by car |
| Sunday | Both home | Wobbles focus day — the week's big adventure |

Layered on top:
- **Rotating activity idea** (1 per day, deterministic): drawn from 4 pools — prep ideas (pre-homecoming), both-home ideas (scatter-feed, recall relay, box maze, mat-settling, cooperative-care minute, hide-and-seek, frozen KONG, trick of the week…), office-day ideas (licky-mat departure, radio, fake-departure reps, puzzle-feeder dinner, puppy-cam check, lobby-bench social, long chew), weekend adventures (big-park trip, dog run, café training, milestone photo, spa morning, carry-adventure).
- **Park night** flag: 7 pm park socialisation every other day (post-homecoming).
- **One-off reminders**: anything you add in Household settings for a specific date, pinned to the top.

## 3. Automatic Nudges (rule-based, max 3 + pinned)

## 4. Printable Checklists (manual tick-lists on the Checklists page)

**Daily** — fresh water (wash + refill) · meals measured & fed (3×/day → 2×) · eye wipe + dry under-eye fur · beard blotted after drinks · toilet trips after every meal/nap/play · 2–5 min training micro-session · one new positive experience · play + enforced nap cycles · body feel-over during a cuddle · tick check (ears, face, neck, toes).

**Weekly** — line brush 3–4 sessions + comb check · S.C.E.N.T. check (Skin, Coat, Ears, Nails, Teeth) · nail grinder ×3 micro-sessions · ear sniff-and-look · weigh-in + log · wash bedding & soft toys · rotate the toy box · refill three frozen KONGs · trim eye-corner hair · check harness fit.

**Monthly** — bath + conditioner + full fluff dry · nail length audit (no clicking on floor) · parasite preventatives given & logged · paw-pad hair trim · sanitary tidy · weight photo + coat photo · restock consumables · book next groom.

(Plus event checklists: Puppy Arrival, Grooming Day, Bath Day, First Vet Visit, etc.)

## 5. Grooming Frequency Cheatsheet (reference table on /grooming)

Quick brush 3–4×/week (daily in coat change) · full line brush weekly · bath + blow-dry fortnightly · home body clip top-up every 3–4 weeks · ear check weekly · nails (grinder) Mon/Wed/Sat · teeth 3×/week · eye wipe daily · professional groom every 6–8 weeks from ~5–6 months.

## 6. Daily Anchors baked into guides (not scheduled, but stated everywhere)

- 7:15 am toilet/morning walk (carry to grass pre-clearance)
- Evening walk after 7 pm sunset; every other day = park social session
- Dinner after the evening walk
- Enforced naps between play cycles
